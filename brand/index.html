Pharoam brand: the Line P seal

The Line P lighthouse inside a seal, with a fine inner ring, the shoreline, and the beam running out past the edge. The seal is the identifier on its own: app icon, favicon, sign-in, documents. In the wordmark, the open line P stands in for the first letter. Colours: Graphite and channel.
