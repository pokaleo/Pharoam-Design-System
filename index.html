Pharoam design system

“The lighthouse does not choose the road. It helps people see it.” Everything here serves that sentence: Pharoam shows what is known, says where it came from, and leaves the choice with the person.

This is the visual language, the domain vocabulary and the components the whole product is built from: Map, Assess, Explore, Learn, Prepare, Settings, and later sharing and the organisation admin. The four sample views (skills, gap, diagnostic results and an AI-led session) show it applied; they illustrate the system and are not final screens.

Paper and Graphite by day: a quiet ground, near-black ink, and one channel blue for what a person has shown.

The lamp room at night: the same marks reversed, and the lamp still lit.

The idea: the P is the lighthouse

The capital P is drawn as a lighthouse in one even line: two tapering tower walls, a dome, one band, and the lamp glowing in the counter of the P. Set in a seal with a fine inner ring, the shoreline and a beam running out past the edge, it becomes the identifier. In the wordmark the open line P takes the place of the first letter, so the name and the lighthouse are the same thing.

  • Warm through the one gold lamp and the serif used for long reading, not through decoration.
  • Calm through restraint: Graphite, Paper and one blue; flat surfaces; nothing that moves to get attention.
  • Trustworthy through candour: every claim shows its source, AI is always labelled, history is never erased. The seal reads as something official.
  • Lasting: a drawn mark and quiet colour age well across the years someone keeps a profile.

Foundations

Every value lives in assets/css/tokens.css. Components read token names only, so light and dark, the two densities and more contrast all come from the same components.

Colour

  • Graphite is the brand. The seal, the wordmark, text and the one filled button on a screen.
  • One hue does the work. Channel blue marks a tested level (what a person showed in a diagnostic), the main data, links and focus. It stays near 2% of a screen.
  • The lamp is graphic only. Gold appears in the logo and as a highlight wash (search hits, selected text, a changed line in a conflict). It never carries text or status.
  • No green anywhere. Nothing in Pharoam passes or fails, so nothing is coloured as passing.
  • Warning colours are kept for real problems. Amber means something needs attention (a missing file, unchecked content). Red means something is wrong (a conflict, a save that did not complete). A gap is never amber or red.
  • Colour is never alone. Every coloured mark has an icon and a word.

Domain vocabulary

The concepts that appear on every screen get one treatment each, everywhere: a word, an icon and a colour, in that order of importance. Every item is shown here in light and dark, and once in the sample views (switch on “Name the vocabulary” there to see where).

The four sources, and evidence

Every level comes from one of four sources, and every label says whether the level was tested. Two are untested claims; two are tested in a diagnostic. The level moves only on what a person shows: claims and evidence decide what a diagnostic asks and what its explanation says, never a tested level.

Self-reportedThe level you give yourself. Untested. Ink, a person.

  2. Otherwise, the latest AI judgement you accepted.

The five levels

Levels are named steps on a 0 to 4 scale, not amounts. The stair glyph lights only the step the person stands on, so it reads as “where”, never “how full”. The name always comes with it; the glyph alone means nothing. On a result card the number may follow the name in grey (“Working level 2”); it never stands alone, and there are no percentages, gauges or progress to 100.

Diagnostic results

A diagnostic is the only thing that produces a tested level. It is adaptive: questions get harder after right answers and easier after wrong ones. Offline it is a multiple-choice question bank, and the result is demonstrated. In AI mode it is a tailored session of multiple-choice, open answers and practical tasks, and the result is AI correlated · after diagnostic. The latest result counts; every one stays in the skill’s history.

Demonstrated Working AI correlated · after diagnostic Working Shown

DemonstratedOffline. Multiple-choice questions starting at your claimed level (or Working), marked by a published rule. Always carries the offline-limit note above, word for word.

ShownMarks the history entry that is the skill’s level. Every other entry stays listed, never hidden.
ExplanationThe written account with an AI-correlated result: what the session showed, how it compares with the evidence, possible reasons for any difference, and a next step.

Gap groups and requirements

A gap is information, not failure. The four groups are neutral: only “supported” carries the channel, because it rests on a tested level, and nothing is amber or red. Groups are sections of a page, never a count or a share. Every requirement shows its one level and the source label of that level.

AI states

Anything an AI made carries a dashed edge, the AI monogram, the model’s name and a disclaimer. It is never verified and never looks like a demonstrated level. The person accepts, adjusts or skips an AI judgement, and the decision stays visible.

Pending Accepted by you Adjusted by you Skipped by you Drafted by AI

An accepted AI judgement is an untested claim. It becomes the level shown only while no diagnostic exists. Adjusting one saves the level you chose as self-reported. There is no AI overall level: a diagnostic’s explanation does that job.

Record states

History is kept. A correction strikes the old value and shows the new one; a retraction strikes the whole record and keeps it in place with the person’s reason. Neither is red: changing your mind is not an error.

One question at a time, of three kinds: multiple-choice, an open answer, or a practical task. A question-bank session is multiple-choice only, in a solid frame; an AI-led session mixes all three. An adaptive session has no fixed length, so progress is the phase it is in (finding your level, checking around it, wrapping up), never a count of questions left, a score or a share. “Finish sooner” asks it to wrap up in fewer questions; it is a request, not a promise, and the note under the phases says so. In an AI-led session the whole frame is dashed and every question the AI wrote is labelled; “I’m not sure” is always an answer.

  • Demonstrated adds the offline-limit note, word for word, and “Get a more accurate result with AI” with its ?. AI correlated adds the explanation, the mismatch callout when the levels differ, the “Considered” list, the model’s name and the disclaimer, and “Run the diagnostic again”.
  • The mismatch callout works both ways: said higher than shown, or shown higher than said. It gives possible reasons, never certainties, then a next step. Encouraging, never accusing, and never amber or red.

Accessibility and languages

WCAG 2.2 AA in both themes and both densities is the floor, not a goal.

  • Never colour alone. Every source, level, state and marker has a word and an icon; tested labels carry the ruler and untested ones do not; Demonstrated and AI correlated differ by the AI monogram and the dashed edge, not by colour; the samples remain fully readable in greyscale.
  • Contrast. All text 4.5:1 or more (table below); input boundaries, the AI edge and the focus ring 3:1 or more.
  • Focus. A 2px ring with a 2px gap in the primary colour, 3px with more contrast. Never removed, never hidden by a sticky bar.
  • Targets. Controls are 32px in compact and 40px in comfortable; nothing interactive is under 24 by 24, including tags that are links.
  • Reduced motion and more contrast follow the system and can be set in Pharoam. More contrast turns grey text to ink and every line to the strong line. In Windows high contrast (forced colours) every tag, panel and AI edge keeps a system-coloured border, AI judgements and AI-correlated results stay dashed, and the level stair keeps its lit step.
  • Structure. One h1 per screen, headings in order, row headers in tables, live regions for queue decisions and sync changes, labels on every icon-only button.

Voice

Plain and kind, to someone who may be unsure about their career. Pharoam describes; the person decides.

PrincipleWriteNot
Say what is known“Two core requirements have nothing in your profile yet.”“You’re missing 2 key skills!”
Gaps are places to go“Under the level asked, for now.”“You failed to meet this requirement.”
Name the source“Quoted from your write-up, paragraph 3.”“Based on your profile.”
Say where a level came from“From your AI diagnostic on 25 Oct.”“Your level.”
AI proposes, you decide“An AI judgement. It counts once you accept it.”“Verified by AI.”
Explain a difference, never accuse“That may be because you haven’t worked on replication recently.”“Your CV overstates your experience.”
Reassure with facts“Nothing has been overwritten.”“Don’t worry!”
No pressure“Decide later.”“Don’t lose your progress! Finish now.”
  • Sentence case, active voice, second person. Short sentences. No exclamation marks.
  • Buttons name what happens. Errors say what happened and what to do next.
  • Dates in words (“14 Sep 2026”); relative only for the last few days (“2 min ago”, “Yesterday”).
  • No streaks, badges, points, confetti, congratulations or reminders that nag.

Glossary of fixed terms

Evidence
A record of something you did: a job, project, repo, degree or certificate. Linked to skills; it carries no level. Lives in its own file.
Skill
Something you can do, at a level, from one or more sources.
Level
Aware, Beginner, Working, Proficient or Expert: the steps 0 to 4. The name always leads; a number only follows it, in grey.
Self-reported · AI judgement · Demonstrated · AI correlated
The four sources of a level. The first two are untested claims; the last two are tested in a diagnostic, and only they support a requirement. Always these words.
Pathway
A kind of role and what it asks for. Not “job”, not “match”.
Gap
The difference between what a pathway asks for and what your profile shows.
Supported · Claimed · Below · Unknown
The four gap groups.
Core · Helpful
How much a pathway needs a requirement.
Diagnostic
The adaptive assessment in Assess that produces a demonstrated or AI-correlated level. Not “test”, “exam” or “check”.
Skill history
Every level a skill has had, from every source, newest first, with the shown one marked.
Learning plan
What you intend to learn toward a pathway. Progress shows in your next diagnostic.
Your folder
The storage you chose. The files there are the master copy.
Pending · Accepted · Adjusted · Skipped · Drafted by AI
The AI states. The person accepts, adjusts or skips.
Explanation
The written account that comes with an AI-correlated result.
Corrected · Retracted
Changes of mind. Both stay in the history.
Content pack
A set of pathways and checks from someone else. Unverified until reviewed.
Activity log
What Pharoam read, sent and wrote, and when.

Words to avoid

Score, rating, percentage, pointsSay the level’s name.
Fit, match, compatible“What the pathway asks for” and “what your profile shows”.
Rank, best, top, recommendedList in the person’s order or A to Z.
Failed, weak, lacking, missing skills“Below the level asked, for now”, “nothing in your profile yet”.
Verified, certified, proven“Demonstrated” or “AI correlated” for a tested result; “AI judgement” for a level AI gave from your evidence.
Evidenced, overall level, “I disagree”, “Dismiss”“Self-reported, with evidence linked”; the diagnostic’s explanation.
Confidence, likelihood, correlation, how sureExplain the reasons in words.
Streak, badge, level up, unlock, achievementNothing. Progress shows in the next diagnostic.
Delete (for history)“Retract” or “correct”. History is kept.
Sync failed, error 409“Two versions of sql.md. Nothing has been overwritten.”
